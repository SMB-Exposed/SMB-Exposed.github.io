About this site

An independent record, built from documents and published reporting.

What this is

This site collects and organises what is publicly documented about one company's leadership, in one place, with sources attached.

It is written and maintained independently. It is not affiliated with any company, competitor or campaign.

How reports are put together

Each report draws on public filings, court records, published news coverage and original documents. Statements of fact carry a numbered footnote linking to where they came from. Analysis and opinion are labelled as such.

Corrections and right of reply

If you believe something on this site is inaccurate, send the specific statement and the evidence. Verified errors are corrected promptly, and the correction is noted at the bottom of the page it appears on.

Anyone named in a report may send a response, which can be added to that report.

Contact details will be added here.
